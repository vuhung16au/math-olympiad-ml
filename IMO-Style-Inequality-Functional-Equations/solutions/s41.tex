\addcontentsline{toc}{subsubsection}{Solution 41. The Prime-Exponent Trap}

\begin{dlsummary}[title={\bfseries Solution 41. The Prime-Exponent Trap},breakable]

\begin{proof}
We prove that the only solution is
\[
\boxed{f(x)=x\qquad(x\in\mathbb Q_{>0}).}
\]

\paragraph{1. Turn the inequality into equality.}

Positivity and multiplicativity give
\[
f(1)=1,\qquad f(1/x)=\frac1{f(x)}.
\]
Applying the inequality at \(1/x\), we obtain
\[
\frac1{f(f(x)^3)}\ge\frac1{x^2f(x)}.
\]
Together with the original inequality, this forces
\[
f(f(x))^3=x^2f(x). \tag{1}
\]

\paragraph{2. Isolate the deviation from the identity.}

Fix \(x>0\), and put
\[
h=\frac{f(x)}x.
\]
By multiplicativity,
\[
f(f(x))=f(xh)=f(x)f(h).
\]
Substituting into (1) gives
\[
f(h)^3=h^{-2}. \tag{2}
\]

We will prove that a positive rational \(h\) satisfying (2) must equal \(1\).

\paragraph{3. Iterate and use prime exponents.}

Set \(h_0=h\) and \(h_{n+1}=f(h_n)\). Applying \(f\) repeatedly to (2), using multiplicativity, gives
\[
h_{n+1}^3=h_n^{-2}\qquad(n\ge0).
\]

For a prime \(p\), let \(e_n\) be the exponent of \(p\) in the prime factorisation of the positive rational \(h_n\). Each \(e_n\) is an integer, possibly negative, and
\[
3e_{n+1}=-2e_n.
\]
Consequently,
\[
3^n e_n=(-2)^n e_0.
\]
Since \(2\) and \(3\) are coprime, \(3^n\) divides \(e_0\) for every \(n\). Therefore \(e_0=0\).

This holds for every prime \(p\), so \(h=1\). Hence \(f(x)=x\) for every positive rational \(x\).

Conversely, \(f(x)=x\) satisfies both conditions, with equality.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Takeaways.}
\begin{itemize}
\item **Reciprocals reverse inequalities:** Multiplicativity makes the substitution \(x\mapsto1/x\) especially powerful.
\item **Normalise the defect:** The ratio \(f(x)/x\) separates the desired solution from its possible deviations.
\item **Iteration creates divisibility:** A relation that looks harmless once can become impossible when repeated indefinitely.
\item **The rational domain matters:** Prime exponents must remain integers at every iteration.
\end{itemize}

\end{dlsummary}
