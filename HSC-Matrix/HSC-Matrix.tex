\section{Introduction}

\begin{remark}[Syllabus Note]
Matrices are not part of the current NSW NESA Mathematics syllabuses. The content in this booklet is adapted from the Queensland (QCAA) Specialist Mathematics syllabus. It serves as an excellent enrichment topic for students interested in linear algebra, computer graphics, and machine learning.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Project Overview}
This booklet is a growing collection of matrix problems. It introduces matrix arithmetic, determinants, inverses, and applications to solving systems of linear equations and geometric transformations.

\subsection{Target Audience}
This resource is written for students who want to explore linear algebra beyond the standard NSW syllabus, in a clear, classroom-friendly format. In particular, this booklet is for:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item NSW Maths Extension 1 and 2 students.
  \item QLD Maths Units 3 and 4 Specialist students who want to refresh their matrix knowledge.
  \item Students all around Australia who want to prepare for their maths studies in university.
\end{itemize}

This booklet may also be beneficial for university units such as:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item USYD MATH1061 Mathematics 1A
  \item UNSW MATH1131 Mathematics 1A, 1B
\end{itemize}

\subsection{How to Use This Booklet}
Read the short summary of the core formulas first, then attempt each problem before reading the solution.

\subsection{Knowledge Needed}
The key background knowledge is:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Basic arithmetic and algebraic manipulation.
  \item Understanding of systems of linear equations.
  \item Basic 2D geometry (for geometric transformations).
\end{itemize}
