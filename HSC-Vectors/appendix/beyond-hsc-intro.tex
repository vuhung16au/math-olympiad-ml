\noindent\textbf{\large Beyond HSC Vectors --- Modern Exts and NSW's 60s Heritage}

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent
Vectors are an important part of senior mathematics across Australia.
While NSW Mathematics Extension~1 and Extension~2 develop vector algebra,
geometry, proofs, equations of lines and curves, other states introduce
some additional techniques.

Moreover, these extensions are \textbf{not foreign to NSW}. Between 1966 and 1980, the highest level of senior mathematics in NSW (originally called \textbf{First Level}, and later \textbf{4~Unit}) was a \textbf{two-year senior course} across Years~11 and~12. Section~4 of the 1965 syllabus was explicitly titled \emph{``Plane Geometry, Geometric Algebra, Matrices, Geometry in Three Dimensions''}. It unified 3D vectors, equations of planes, determinants, and linear dependence. When 4~Unit was compressed into a 1-year Year~12 course in 1981, these topics were removed. Exploring them today reconnects students with the complete mathematical picture that once anchored NSW senior mathematics.

This section explores five ideas:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Polar form of two-dimensional vectors}
        (QLD Specialist Mathematics --- Unit~1)
  \item \textbf{The cross product}
        (VIC Specialist Mathematics --- Units~3--4;
         QLD Specialist Mathematics --- Unit~3;
         HSC First Level 1966--1980;
         including applications to 2D polygon and parametric curve areas)
  \item \textbf{Equations of planes in three dimensions}
        (VIC Specialist Mathematics --- Units~3--4;
         QLD Specialist Mathematics --- Unit~3;
         HSC 4~Unit 1966--1980)
  \item \textbf{Linear dependence and independence}
        (VIC Specialist Mathematics --- Units~3--4;
         HSC First Level 1966--1980)
  \item \textbf{Vector-valued motion in three dimensions}
        (VIC Specialist Mathematics --- Units~3--4;
         QLD Specialist Mathematics --- Unit~3)
\end{enumerate}
