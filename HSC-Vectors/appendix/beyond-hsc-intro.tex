\textit{\textbf{Enrichment material.}
The five appendices that follow explore ideas from Victorian and
Queensland Specialist Mathematics.
They are intended as enrichment for NSW students studying the
NESA 2024 syllabuses, \textbf{not} as additional HSC examination
requirements.
They also provide a gentle introduction to ideas encountered in
first-year university mathematics, physics, engineering and computer
science.}

\noindent\textbf{\large Beyond HSC Vectors --- Ideas from Victoria and Queensland}

\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent
Vectors are an important part of senior mathematics across Australia.
While NSW Mathematics Extension~1 and Extension~2 develop vector algebra,
geometry, proofs, equations of lines and curves, other states introduce
some additional techniques.

This section explores five ideas:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Polar form of two-dimensional vectors}
        (QLD Specialist Mathematics --- Unit~1)
  \item \textbf{The cross product}
        (VIC Specialist Mathematics --- Units~3--4;
         QLD Specialist Mathematics --- Unit~3;
         including applications to 2D polygon and parametric curve areas)
  \item \textbf{Equations of planes in three dimensions}
        (VIC Specialist Mathematics --- Units~3--4;
         QLD Specialist Mathematics --- Unit~3)
  \item \textbf{Linear dependence and independence}
        (VIC Specialist Mathematics --- Units~3--4)
  \item \textbf{Vector-valued motion in three dimensions}
        (VIC Specialist Mathematics --- Units~3--4;
         QLD Specialist Mathematics --- Unit~3)
\end{enumerate}
